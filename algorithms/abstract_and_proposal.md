5 of the most important algorithms humans use

A quick and compelling exploration of five important algorithms discovered and used by humans.

Are you a human, or do you at least have genes? Congratulations! You've benefited from an algorithm! In this talk, we'll look at five of the most important algorithms humans have discovered - what they are, why they matter, how they work, and how to get more from them. We'll start our list with genetic algorithms - inspired by evolution, used by trillions! Next we'll cover the odd-even merge sort because (1) I love recursion and (2) hugely simplifying and (3) did I mention I love recursion? (see number 1). Thirdly, we'll talk about Google's PageRank algorithm. Fourth is a consensus algorithm called Paxos used in computing clusters. Lastly, we'll talk through backpropagation in neural networks.

In many ways this is a "history of programming" talk and I'm an old-timer who started programming in 1980, has shipped code in 36 programming languages, has co-created a programming language, and really enjoys talking about the parts of programming that change slowly - like algorithms. Seriously, I love talking about this stuff. I'm also principal engineer at Axios Media (axios.com).
