Session: Your Next Career (or, what to do after the robots take over)

Short description: A ever-so-very-serious look at the job prospects for the 4.4 million of us who write code the old fashioned way, by being a human rather than an AI.

Long description: Let's hypothetically say that ChatCoAIPilotMicroOpenSoftBotGPT decides that doing this programming gig isn't so hard after all and it would like to take over that field. Let's also say that you and I are "displaced" by this shift in how programming is done and we find ourselves. Whatever will we do? I'm not ready to retire and I'm going to need a new career!

This talk looks at who we are, what we're good at, and where we can work without worrying about the robots taking those jobs too. We're problem solvers, and we're creative - we can figure this out! (Hmmm, maybe job placement counselor should be on the list...)

Spoiler alert: you're probably looking at a pay cut!
